🔍 Threads Pain Scanner

Privacy Policy • Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Threads Pain Scanner ("the App") is a research tool that helps analyze public posts from Meta Threads to identify user pain points and generate app ideas. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Data We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect:

  • Search Queries: Keywords you enter to search Threads posts
  • Threads API Data: Public posts retrieved via Meta Threads API based on your searches
  • Analysis Results: Insights generated by AI analysis of retrieved posts

We do not collect personal information, login credentials, or private messages.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:

  • Perform keyword searches on Threads
  • Analyze posts using local or cloud-based AI
  • Generate reports and export results

4. Data Storage

The App runs locally on your computer. Search results and analysis are stored temporarily in your browser session and are not persisted on any external servers unless you explicitly export them.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Meta Threads API: To search and retrieve public posts (Threads Privacy Policy)
  • Ollama (optional): Local AI processing on your machine
  • OpenAI/Anthropic/xAI (optional): Cloud AI analysis if configured

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. Search queries are sent only to the APIs you configure (Threads API and your chosen LLM provider).

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Stop using the App at any time
  • Delete any exported data from your device
  • Revoke API access tokens via Meta Developer Portal

8. Security

We implement reasonable security measures. API tokens are stored locally in your environment files and are never shared. We recommend keeping your tokens secure and rotating them regularly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
