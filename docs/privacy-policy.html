Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 21, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Threads Pain Scanner ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

The App accesses the following data through the Threads API:

  • Public Threads Posts: We search and analyze publicly available posts based on keywords you provide.
  • Post Metadata: Username, timestamp, and post content of public posts.

We do NOT collect:

  • Your personal Threads account information
  • Private messages or non-public content
  • Your location or device information

2. How We Use Information

The data accessed is used solely for:

  • Displaying search results based on your keyword queries
  • Analyzing public sentiment and trends using AI
  • Generating insights about user needs and pain points for market research

3. Data Storage

  • Search results are processed locally on your device
  • AI analysis is performed locally using Ollama (no data sent to external AI services)
  • We do not store or retain any Threads data on external servers
  • Exported files (JSON/CSV) are saved locally on your device

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties. All processing happens locally on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Meta Threads API: To search public posts. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Ollama (Local): For AI analysis, running entirely on your local machine.

6. Your Rights

Since we don't store your personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. You can stop using the App at any time.

7. Security

The App uses secure HTTPS connections for API requests. Your Threads API access token is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
